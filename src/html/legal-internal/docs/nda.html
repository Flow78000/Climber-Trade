1. Parties

This Non-Disclosure Agreement is entered into between:

  • Disclosing Party: Climber Trade, represented at www.climbertrade.com
  • Receiving Party: The Beta Tester (the individual granted access to the beta testing program)

By participating in the Climber Trade Beta Testing Program, the Receiving Party acknowledges and agrees to all terms set forth in this agreement.

2. Purpose

The purpose of this NDA is to protect proprietary and confidential information disclosed to Beta Testers during their participation in the Climber Trade Beta Testing Program. This agreement ensures that all platform-related information shared during the beta phase remains strictly confidential.

3. Definition of Confidential Information

Confidential Information includes, without limitation, all information related to the Climber Trade platform that is disclosed to the Receiving Party during the beta testing program, whether in written, oral, electronic, or any other form. This encompasses:

  • Platform features, functionality, and user interface designs
  • CLIMB Score algorithms, metrics, and scoring methodologies
  • Technical architecture, APIs, and infrastructure details
  • Business models, pricing strategies, and commercial terms
  • Trading regulations, internal processes, and operational procedures
  • Pool of liquidity mechanics and allocation formulas
  • Marketing materials, launch strategies, and roadmap details
  • Any bugs, errors, or issues discovered during testing
  • Internal communications between Climber Trade team members, partners, and Beta Testers
  • Financial data, revenue figures, cost structures, and commercial performance indicators
  • Any other information identified as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential

4. Obligations of the Receiving Party

The Receiving Party agrees to:

  1. Maintain strict confidentiality of all Confidential Information received
  2. Not disclose, publish, or disseminate any Confidential Information to any third party, whether individual or entity
  3. Not reproduce or copy Confidential Information except as strictly necessary for beta testing activities
  4. Not use Confidential Information for any purpose other than participating in the beta testing program
  5. Take all reasonable measures to prevent unauthorized access to or disclosure of Confidential Information
  6. Not post, share, or reference any Confidential Information on social media, forums, messaging platforms, or any public or semi-public channels
  7. Immediately notify Climber Trade of any unauthorized disclosure or suspected breach

5. Scope of Covered Information

This NDA covers all information obtained through:

  • Direct access to the Climber Trade platform during beta testing
  • Documentation, guides, or manuals provided to Beta Testers
  • Communications with Climber Trade team members
  • Group discussions, feedback sessions, or testing meetings
  • Screenshots, recordings, or any captures of platform content
  • Internal metrics, data, or performance indicators observed during testing

6. Duration

The confidentiality obligations under this NDA are indefinite. They survive the termination of the beta testing program and the Receiving Party's relationship with Climber Trade. There is no expiration date on the duty of confidentiality.

7. Authorized Communication Channels

All communications regarding the beta testing program, feedback, bug reports, and any matters related to Confidential Information must be conducted exclusively through:

Email: contact@climbertrade.com. No other communication channel is authorized for the exchange of Confidential Information. The Receiving Party must not use personal email, messaging apps, social media, or any other medium to discuss or transmit Confidential Information.

8. Exceptions

The obligations of confidentiality do not apply to information that:

  • Was already publicly available at the time of disclosure, without any breach by the Receiving Party
  • Becomes publicly available after disclosure through no fault of the Receiving Party
  • Was independently developed by the Receiving Party without reference to the Confidential Information
  • Is disclosed pursuant to a valid court order or legal requirement, provided the Receiving Party notifies Climber Trade promptly and cooperates to limit the scope of disclosure

9. Breach & Sanctions

Any breach of this NDA will result in the following consequences, which may be applied cumulatively:

9.1 Immediate Exclusion

The Receiving Party will be immediately and permanently excluded from the Beta Testing Program and all Climber Trade services. All access will be revoked without notice.

9.2 Civil Proceedings

Climber Trade reserves the right to pursue civil action seeking compensatory damages for any losses, including but not limited to financial losses, reputational harm, and loss of competitive advantage resulting from the breach.

9.3 Criminal Prosecution

Where applicable under local and international law, Climber Trade reserves the right to file criminal complaints for misappropriation of trade secrets, unauthorized disclosure of confidential business information, or any other applicable offense.

9.4 Damages & Indemnification

The Receiving Party agrees to indemnify Climber Trade for all direct and indirect damages resulting from a breach of this NDA, including legal fees, investigative costs, and any third-party claims arising from the unauthorized disclosure.

10. Return of Materials

Upon termination of the beta testing program, or upon request by Climber Trade, the Receiving Party must:

  • Return all physical materials containing Confidential Information
  • Permanently delete all digital copies, files, screenshots, and recordings related to the platform
  • Confirm in writing that all Confidential Information has been returned or destroyed

11. Governing Law

This NDA shall be governed by and construed in accordance with applicable law. Any dispute arising from or related to this agreement shall be submitted to the competent courts. The Receiving Party consents to the jurisdiction of such courts for the purpose of any proceedings related to this NDA.

12. Contact

For any questions regarding this NDA or the Beta Testing Program: contact@climbertrade.com
