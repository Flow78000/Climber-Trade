1. Object

Climber Trade offers two distinct programs designed to reward community growth and align incentives between the platform, its traders, and content creators who help build the ecosystem.

  • Referral Program — available to all registered traders. Provides subscription discounts for both the referrer and the referred trader.
  • Premium Affiliation Program — designed for content creators, trading educators, and community leaders. Provides recurring revenue tied to the long-term success of affiliated traders.

Both programs are governed by these Terms and Conditions and are subject to the general Terms of Service of Climber Trade. Participation in either program implies full acceptance of the terms outlined herein.

2. Referral Program

The Referral Program is open to all traders with an active Climber Trade account. It operates through unique referral codes and links that can be shared freely.

2.1 Referrer Benefits

  • -$10/month discount on the referrer's subscription for each active referral
  • Duration: 6 months fixed per referral, starting from the referral's first subscription payment
  • Cumulative: discounts stack with each qualifying referral
  • Maximum impact: 20 active referrals = FREE subscription ($10 × 20 = $200/month discount on the $199/month Standard plan)
  • Beyond 20 referrals: additional credits are stored on the referrer's account balance

2.2 Referred Trader Benefits

  • -20% discount on the first month of the Standard subscription
  • Standard plan: $199 → $159 for the first month
  • Discount applies automatically when the referral code or link is used during registration

2.3 Referral Codes & Links

Code format: CLIMB-XXXXX
Link format: climbertrade.com/ref/XXXXX

Each trader receives a unique referral code upon account creation. The code can be shared via any channel. Tracking is automatic — the referral is attributed at the time of the referred trader's first payment.

2.4 Activation Conditions

  • A referral is considered active only when the referred trader maintains a paying subscription
  • If the referred trader cancels or does not renew, the corresponding $10/month discount ceases at the end of the referred trader's paid period
  • Self-referral or referral of accounts controlled by the same person is strictly prohibited

3. Premium Affiliation Program

The Premium Affiliation Program is reserved for content creators, trading educators, community managers, and individuals with an established audience who can drive meaningful growth to the platform.

3.1 Recurring Subscription Commission

  • 15% recurring commission on every subscription payment made by each affiliated trader
  • Duration: lifetime — as long as the affiliated trader remains a paying subscriber
  • Applied to each billing cycle (monthly renewals)

3.2 Payout Commission

  • 1% commission on every payout earned by the affiliated trader in the Live Trading Program
  • This commission is paid by Climber Trade from its own share — it does not reduce the trader's earnings

3.3 Calculation Example

Scenario: An affiliated trader generates $10,000 in profits during a payout cycle with a 60/40 split (Elite tier).

• Trader receives: $10,000 × 60% = $6,000
• Climber Trade receives: $10,000 × 40% = $4,000
• Affiliate commission: $6,000 × 1% = $60 (paid from Climber's $4,000 share)

The trader's payout is not affected — the affiliate commission comes entirely from Climber Trade's portion.

3.4 No Cap

There is no cap on affiliate earnings. Commission grows naturally with the number and success of affiliated traders. The more traders an affiliate brings and the better those traders perform, the higher the affiliate's recurring income.

3.5 Eligibility

  • Application required — submit via the Climber Trade dashboard or by contacting the team
  • Approval is at the sole discretion of Climber Trade
  • Ideal candidates: trading educators, YouTube/social media creators, community leaders, trading signal providers, fund managers

4. Non-Cumulative Clause

The Referral Program and the Premium Affiliation Program are mutually exclusive. A participant may only be enrolled in one program at a time.

  • A trader using the Referral Program cannot simultaneously participate in the Premium Affiliation Program, and vice versa
  • Switching between programs is possible upon written request, but active benefits from the previous program will cease upon transition
  • A referred trader who later becomes an affiliate must choose one program and forfeit the other

5. Tracking & Payment

5.1 Dashboard

Both referrers and affiliates have access to a dedicated section in their Climber Trade dashboard providing real-time visibility on:

  • Number of active referrals or affiliated traders
  • Current subscription discount (referrers) or accumulated commissions (affiliates)
  • Referral code usage history and conversion metrics
  • Payout history and pending commissions

5.2 Payment Processing

  • Referral discounts are applied automatically to the next billing cycle
  • Affiliate commissions are calculated at each payout cycle and paid through the standard withdrawal process via RISE WORKS
  • Minimum payout threshold for affiliate commissions: $50
  • Settlement follows the same J+3 to J+5 business day timeline as trader payouts

6. Termination

Participation in either program may be terminated under the following conditions:

6.1 Voluntary Termination

  • Either party may terminate participation at any time by written notice
  • Outstanding commissions or discounts owed at the time of termination will be honored through the end of the current billing cycle

6.2 Termination by Climber Trade

Climber Trade reserves the right to immediately terminate participation and revoke all benefits in case of:

  • Fraudulent or misleading marketing practices
  • Spam, unsolicited mass messaging, or deceptive advertising
  • Misrepresentation of Climber Trade's services, returns, or guarantees
  • Self-referral schemes or creation of fake accounts to generate referral benefits
  • Violation of the Climber Trade Terms of Service or Trading Regulations
  • Any activity that damages Climber Trade's reputation or brand

6.3 Consequences of Termination

  • All future commissions and discounts are forfeited upon termination for cause
  • Existing referred/affiliated traders remain Climber Trade clients but are no longer linked to the terminated participant
  • Climber Trade's decision on termination is final and not subject to appeal
