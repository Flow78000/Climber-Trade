1. Overview

The Climber Trade Liquidity Pool is the central mechanism through which real trading capital is allocated to funded traders. It is designed around three core principles:

  • Self-regulating \u2014 the pool cannot be exhausted thanks to the automatic Cohort Factor (FC) that scales allocations inversely with the number of qualifying traders
  • Merit-based \u2014 capital allocation is determined algorithmically by the trader's CLIMB Score, with higher-scoring traders receiving proportionally larger allocations
  • Transparent \u2014 pool size, distribution, and allocation parameters are visible to all traders through a public dashboard

Capital allocated through the pool is real. There is no fixed capital guarantee \u2014 allocation is always computed dynamically based on the pool's current size, the trader's score, and the total cohort.

2. Pool Funding Sources

The Liquidity Pool is funded from two revenue streams:

2.1 Subscription Revenue

  • 50% of all evaluation subscription revenues (Standard plan at $199/month) are allocated to the pool
  • This includes both new subscriptions and monthly renewals

2.2 Live Trading Commission

  • 50% of Climber Trade's commission on live trader profits is reinvested into the pool
  • This creates a virtuous cycle: successful traders grow the pool, which in turn funds more capital for all funded traders

2.3 Funding Example

Scenario: A live trader requests a $1,000 withdrawal. Trader receives: $1,000 \u00d7 50% = $500 (based on 50/50 profit split). Climber Trade receives: $500. Pool reinvestment: $500 \u00d7 50% = $250 flows back into the Liquidity Pool. The remaining $250 of Climber Trade's share covers operational costs, platform development, and business expenses.

3. Cohort Definition

The statistical cohort determines the denominator used in pool protection calculations. It includes all traders who have demonstrated a minimum level of competence:

N_cohorte = Total traders with CLIMB Score ≥ 50
  • Includes both evaluation-phase traders and live traders
  • Each qualifying trader counts as exactly 1 unit regardless of status
  • The cohort is recalculated in real time as scores change
  • Traders below score 50 are excluded from the cohort count
Important distinction: The cohort (N_cohorte) is used for pool protection calibration. It is a separate concept from the number of funded (live) traders. A trader entering the cohort at score 50 does not receive capital \u2014 they only become part of the statistical denominator.

4. Factor Cohorte (FC) \u2014 Auto-Protection

The Factor Cohorte is an automatic protection mechanism that prevents the pool from being over-allocated regardless of how many traders qualify. It inversely scales each trader's allocation as the cohort grows.

FC = 1 / (1 + (N_cohorte / 50))

4.1 Behavior

  • When the cohort is small, FC approaches 1.0 \u2014 each trader receives a larger share of the pool
  • When the cohort grows, FC decreases \u2014 individual allocations shrink proportionally
  • The pool can never be exhausted because FC ensures total allocations always remain within bounds

5. Score-Based Allocation (%Score)

Each funded trader's base share of the pool is determined by their CLIMB Score using a linear function:

%Score(S) = 1% + ((S - 85) / 15) × 1%

This maps scores in the funded range (85\u2013100) to a pool share between 1.00% and 2.00%:

6. Performance Prime

An additional performance multiplier rewards traders who exceed the minimum funded threshold of 85. The prime scales linearly from 0% at score 85 to a maximum of 10% at score 95 and above:

Prime(S) = min(10%, max(0, (S - 85) × 1%))

7. Seniority Bonus

Traders who maintain their live status over time receive an incremental seniority bonus, rewarding consistency and long-term commitment to the platform:

  • Bonus increases monthly while the trader remains in the Live Trading Program
  • Capped at a maximum of +3% after 6 months of continuous live trading
  • Incremental: approximately +0.5% per month of live tenure
  • Resets if the trader returns to evaluation phase (score degradation or drawdown breach)

8. Final Allocation Formula

The complete allocation for any funded trader is computed by combining all factors:

Alloc(S) = Pool_total × %Score(S) × FC × (1 + Prime(S) + Seniority_Bonus)

9. Elite vs Standard Pool Structure

The total Liquidity Pool is divided into two tiers to reward exceptional performance:

Pool TierAllocationEligibilityDetails
Standard Pool90%CLIMB Score ≥ 85Accessible to all funded traders
Elite Pool10%CLIMB Score ≥ 95Reserved for top-tier traders only

9.1 Double Allocation for Elite Traders

Traders with a CLIMB Score of 95 or above benefit from a double allocation: they receive their standard allocation from the Standard Pool (90%) and an additional allocation from the Elite Pool (10%). This rewards sustained excellence and provides a strong incentive for traders to maintain top-tier performance.

9.2 Distribution within Each Pool

  • Within each pool tier, capital is distributed using the same formula (section 8) applied to the respective pool size
  • The Elite Pool is shared exclusively among traders scoring 95 or higher
  • As fewer traders qualify for Elite status, each Elite trader's share of that reserved 10% is proportionally larger

10. Public Dashboard

In keeping with Climber Trade's commitment to full transparency, the Liquidity Pool is visible to all traders \u2014 both in evaluation and live \u2014 through a public dashboard displaying:

  • Total pool size in real time
  • Number of funded traders currently active in live
  • Cohort size (N_cohorte) and current FC value
  • Anonymized distribution of capital across score brackets
  • Historical pool growth over time
  • Monthly inflows and outflows (subscriptions, commissions, payouts)

The dashboard is accessible from the Wallet & Liquidity Pool section of the trading platform. No trader can see another trader's individual allocation \u2014 only aggregate statistics are displayed.
