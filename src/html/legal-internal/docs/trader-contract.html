1. Object & Scope

This contract governs the relationship between Climber Trade and the Trader upon activation of the Funded Trading Program. By accepting this contract, the Trader agrees to the terms outlined herein and in the associated Trading Regulations.

The Funded Trading Program provides qualified traders with access to real capital from the Climber Trade Liquidity Pool, enabling them to trade on live markets and share in the profits generated. Traders must have successfully completed the Incubation and Scoring phases before entering the Funded Phase.

2. Nature of the Relationship

The relationship between Climber Trade and the Trader is qualified as a service provision agreement. This contract does not create an employment relationship, partnership, or agency.

  • The Trader operates as an independent service provider, not a salaried employee
  • The Trader is classified as an analyst providing market analysis services to Climber Trade
  • The relationship is non-exclusive \u2014 the Trader may engage in other professional activities
  • The Trader is solely responsible for their own social contributions, insurance, and legal obligations

3. Eligibility & Activation

Access to the Funded Trading Program requires all of the following conditions to be met simultaneously:

  1. CLIMB Score \u2265 85/100 \u2014 maintained continuously
  2. 7 consecutive trading days at or above the qualifying score (days with at least 1 trade executed)
  3. 30 calendar days minimum since account creation (Incubation period completed)
  4. KYC identity verification fully validated
Activation process: Eligibility is detected automatically by the system. The Trader receives notification via email and dashboard. After KYC validation, Funded access is activated within 24\u201348 hours (weekdays only, excluding Friday evenings and weekends). Each trader passes to Funded individually and instantly upon meeting all criteria.

4. Capital Allocation

Capital is allocated from the Climber Trade Liquidity Pool using the following formula:

Allocation = Pool_total \u00d7 %Score(S) \u00d7 FC \u00d7 (1 + Prime(S) + Seniority_Bonus). Where %Score ranges from 1.00% (score 85) to 2.00% (score 100), FC is the Cohort Factor providing automatic protection, Prime adds up to +10% for scores above 85, and Seniority Bonus adds up to +3% after 6 months in Funded Phase.

The pool is divided into two tiers:

  • Standard Pool (90%): Accessible to all traders with score \u2265 85
  • Elite Pool (10%): Reserved for traders with score \u2265 95 (who receive a double allocation from both pools)

6. Maintenance Requirements

To maintain Funded trading status and profit-sharing eligibility, the Trader must comply with the following:

  • CLIMB Score \u2265 75 minimum to retain the profit split
  • Minimum activity: at least 4 trading days per rolling 30-day period
  • First payout eligibility: 7 days in Funded Phase before the first withdrawal request
  • Compliance with all Trading Regulations (instruments, position limits, prohibited practices)

7. Scaling Mechanism

Capital allocation is reviewed monthly through an automatic scaling mechanism:

  • Score \u2265 95: +10% of the pool increase shared among traders in this bracket
  • Score 85\u201399: 90% of the pool shared proportionally among qualifying traders
  • Score < 85: No scaling \u2014 0% increase

There is no cap on capital allocation. The pool grows with revenue from subscriptions (50%) and Climber Trade's share of Funded trading profits (50%).

8. Withdrawals & Payouts

All earnings and wallet balances are denominated in ClimbCoin (CC), the platform's internal accounting currency. ClimbCoin is pegged 1:1 to USD. All payouts and settlements are processed in USD via RISE WORKS.

8.1 Automatic Payout Cycles

  • 5th of each month: First automatic cycle
  • 20th of each month: Second automatic cycle
  • On-demand: Daily withdrawal requests with 24-hour advance notice

8.2 Payout Conditions

  • Minimum withdrawal amount: $500
  • Calculation: Processed on trade day +1 (J+1)
  • Settlement: Wire transfer within J+3 to J+5 business days
  • Method: Climber Trade Wallet + RISE WORKS exclusively
  • Only closed and consolidated positions are eligible (open profitable positions are excluded)

8.3 Payout Processing

  • With withdrawal request: Trader's share is paid out (if \u2265 $500), Climber's share is retained automatically
  • Without withdrawal request: Trader's share remains on the account (increasing available capital), Climber's share is retained automatically

9. Intellectual Property

9.1 Trader's Property

Trading strategies developed and employed by the Trader remain the exclusive intellectual property of the Trader. Climber Trade will not claim ownership, reproduce, or distribute the Trader's strategies. A mutual confidentiality agreement protects the Trader's strategic methods.

9.2 Climber Trade's Property

All metrics, scoring data, CLIMB Score calculations, platform algorithms, trading analytics, and aggregated performance data are the exclusive property of Climber Trade. This includes but is not limited to:

  • CLIMB Score system and all associated metrics
  • Anonymized or aggregated trading data used for platform improvement
  • Leaderboard rankings and public performance displays
  • Pool allocation algorithms and cohort calculations

10. KYC & Compliance

The Trader is required to complete Know Your Customer (KYC) identity verification before Funded Phase activation. The Trader must:

  • Provide valid government-issued identification
  • Provide proof of address
  • Complete any additional verification requested by Climber Trade or its compliance partners
  • Keep all personal information up to date \u2014 notify Climber Trade within 14 days of any material change

11. Tax Obligations

Income generated through the Funded Trading Program is classified as non-salaried professional income. The Trader is solely and entirely responsible for:

  • Declaring all income to the relevant tax authorities in their jurisdiction
  • Paying all applicable taxes, social contributions, and regulatory fees
  • Maintaining proper accounting records

Tax attestations are provided via RISE WORKS for the Trader's records.

13. Termination

This contract may be terminated under the following circumstances:

  • By the Trader: At any time, by written request to Climber Trade. Outstanding profits will be settled in the next payout cycle.
  • By Climber Trade: In case of violation of Trading Regulations, fraudulent activity, breach of this contract, or failure to maintain minimum requirements.
  • Automatic termination: Upon permanent exclusion following the sanctions escalation process (warning \u2192 suspension \u2192 permanent ban).

Upon termination, outstanding profits owed to the Trader will be calculated and paid within the standard settlement period, subject to any deductions for violations or damages.
