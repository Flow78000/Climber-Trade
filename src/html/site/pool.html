Score-Based Allocation

Capital is distributed algorithmically based on each trader's CLIMB Score, the FC factor, seniority bonus, and performance prime.

Factor Cohorte (Auto-Protection)
FC = 1 / (1 + (N_cohorte / 50))
Where N_cohorte = total traders with Score ≥ 50. Automatically reduces allocations as more traders qualify, protecting the pool at any scale.
Score Allocation %
%Score(S) = 1% + ((S - 85) / 15) × 1%
Maps a score range of 85–100 to a pool percentage of 1.00%–2.00%. Higher score = proportionally larger allocation.
Performance Prime
Prime(S) = min(10%, max(0, (S - 85) × 1%))
Bonus multiplier from 0% (score 85) to 10% (score 95+). Rewards sustained excellence with additional capital.
Final Allocation
Alloc = Pool × %Score × FC × (1 + Prime + Seniority)
Complete formula combining all factors. Seniority bonus accrues monthly, capped at +3% after 6 months in Live.
