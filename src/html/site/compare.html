The Industry Problem

What's Wrong With Traditional Prop Firms

Three structural issues that work against traders

Profit From Failures

Most prop firms make money from failed evaluations, not from trading profits. Their incentive is for you to fail and rebuy. When 90%+ of traders fail, the business model depends on that failure rate continuing.

Arbitrary Rules

Hidden daily loss limits, time pressure, and artificial constraints designed to trip you up. Many firms use trailing drawdowns, consistency rules, and minimum day requirements that have nothing to do with real trading skill.

No Real Capital

Many firms never allocate real capital. You trade on simulated accounts even after passing the evaluation. The "funded account" is still a demo with payouts coming from evaluation fees collected from other traders.
