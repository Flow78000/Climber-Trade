The CLIMB Score is a composite rating out of 100 based on 16+ metrics organized into 4 pillars. Risk Management (×4): drawdown control, recovery speed after losses, and leverage optimization. Exceptional performances early on are weighted less than sustained results over 200+ days of active trading.

Scoring System
The Risk Management pillar carries a ×4 weight and includes 4 metrics. ABS_NPL (Daily P&L Score): scored daily with a cumulative average; sweet spot at +1%/day (100 pts), decreases both above and below. INPL (Initial Drawdown): measures the deepest loss before first equity high; loss > 10% = 0 pts. R_T_AFD (Recovery Time After Drawdown): granular scoring across 9 drawdown tiers and day-by-day recovery tracking. CT4 (Average Leverage): 2×–5× = 100 pts (optimal zone). Informational metrics like MDD, VAR, and IMDD are also displayed but do not factor into the score.
The Performance pillar carries a ×4 weight and includes 3 metrics. Profit Factor: gross gains divided by gross losses, scored on a curve from 0 (PF < 0.50) to 100 (PF ≥ 4.00), multiplied by a confidence coefficient based on trade count (200+ trades = full ×1.00 confidence, under 50 trades = ×0.25). Sharpe Ratio: provides a bonus of up to +10 points on top of your Profit Factor score, using a risk-free rate of 0. Annualized Yield (YIELD_Y): projects annual return with 50/100 = the 8% market benchmark; full confidence reached at 180 trading days.
The Consistency & Trading Style pillar carries a ×3 weight and includes 3 metrics. Win Rate (CT1): scores range from −100 (0–3%) to +100 (100%); anything below 46% is negative. A confidence factor builds over 22 trading days. Days in Gain (CT2): a direct 1:1 mapping — 60% green days = 60 pts. Lot Stability Ratio (LSR/CT5): average lots per trade divided by standard deviation of lots; LSR ≥ 4.0 = 100 pts, indicating very consistent position sizing. This pillar rewards traders who demonstrate repeatable, disciplined behavior.
Quality Execution is not a weighted pillar like the other three — it acts as a multiplier between ×0.90 and ×1.10 applied to the weighted average of Risk, Performance, and Consistency. A score of 50 = neutral (×1.00). The four metrics are: Return Symmetry (Skew/OF1): lower skew is better, starts at 100. Return Stability (Kurtosis/OF2): lower kurtosis earns higher scores. Extreme Day Impact (OF3): ratio of max extreme day to average daily P&L. Best/Worst Day Ratio (OF4): best day divided by absolute worst day, ratio ≥ 4.30 = 100 pts. Example: 3-pillar average of 78.5 with quality score of 85 → multiplier ×1.07 → final score = 83.9.
The final CLIMB Score formula is: Score = [(Risk × 4 + Performance × 4 + Consistency × 3) / 11] × (1 + (Quality − 50) / 500). The denominator is 11, which is the sum of the pillar coefficients (4 + 4 + 3). The Quality Execution component generates a ratio between 0.90 and 1.10 that adjusts the final score up or down by a maximum of 10%. Negative metric scores are included in calculations but displayed to the trader with a floor of 0. The score updates continuously in real time with a daily consolidation snapshot at end of day.
Funding & Payouts
The pool is funded by two sources: 50% of all subscription revenues and 50% of Climber Trade's share on live trader profits. The statistical cohorte includes all traders with a Score ≥ 50 (both evaluation and live). The Factor Cohorte (FC) = 1 / (1 + (N / 50)) automatically regulates allocation as the trader population grows. Each trader's pool share (%Score) ranges from 1% at score 85 to 2% at score 100. A performance prime of up to +10% is added for scores above 85. The pool is split into an Elite tier (10%, for traders ≥ 95) and a Standard tier (90%, for traders ≥ 85). Elite traders receive dual allocation from both tiers. The pool size and distribution are displayed publicly in real time on the dashboard.
Profit splits are tied directly to your CLIMB Score. Score 95–100 (Elite): 60% trader / 40% Climber. Score 90–94 (Expert): 55% / 45%. Score 85–89 (Standard): 50% / 50%. Score 80–84 (Watch): 45% / 55%. Score 75–79 (Warning): 40% / 60%. Below 75: 0% payout — you have 7 days to recover above 75 before being returned to evaluation. A minimum CLIMB Score of 75 is required to maintain your split, along with regular activity (at least 4 days per 30) and 7 days of live trading before first payout eligibility.
Payouts follow automatic cycles on the 5th and 20th of each month. You can also request a daily on-demand withdrawal with 24 hours advance notice. The minimum withdrawal amount is $500. Only closed, consolidated positions are eligible for withdrawal — open profitable positions are not included. New live traders must complete 7 days of live trading before their first payout eligibility.
You continue trading as long as your CLIMB Score stays above 75.
