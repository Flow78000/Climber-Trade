Your score is calculated across 4 pillars, weighted and combined into a single number out of 100.

Pillar 3
Consistency & Style
coefficient ×3

Quality Execution
multiplier ×0.90–1.10
Return symmetry, kurtosis, extreme day impact. Adjusts final score ±10%.

Score = [(Risk × 4 + Performance × 4 + Consistency × 3) / 11] × Quality Multiplier (0.90–1.10)
