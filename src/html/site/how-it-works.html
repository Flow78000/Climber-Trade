Common Questions

Quick Answers About the Process

What if my score drops below 85 after I get funded?
You stay funded as long as your score remains at 75 or above. Between 75 and 84, your profit split is reduced but you keep trading with real capital. If your score drops below 75 for 7 consecutive days, you return to the Scoring phase to rebuild. You can always get funded again once your score is back at 85+ for 7 trading days.
Is there a time limit on the Scoring phase?
No. Your Standard subscription renews monthly and your Scoring phase has no deadline. The only requirement is 30 calendar days minimum and reaching 85+. Some traders get funded in their first month; others take longer to refine their approach. There is no penalty for taking your time.
How much capital will I actually manage when funded?
Your allocation is a percentage of the liquidity pool, calculated from your score, the pool size, and the number of qualified traders. A score of 85 gives you 1% of the pool; 100 gives you 2%. Bonuses for seniority (up to +3%) and Elite status (up to +10%) increase your share over time. The pool dashboard is public, so you can see the numbers before you even subscribe.
