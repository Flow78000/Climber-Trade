1. Data Controller

Climber Trade is the data controller for all personal data collected through the platform. Contact: contact@climbertrade.com

2. Data Collected

  • Account data: Name, email, phone number, date of birth, country of residence
  • KYC data: Government-issued ID, proof of address, selfie verification (processed securely)
  • Trading data: All trades, positions, P&L, metrics, CLIMB Score history
  • Payment data: Card details processed by our payment provider (not stored on our servers)
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, device information, session data

3. Purpose of Processing

  • Account management and authentication
  • Evaluation scoring and live trading eligibility
  • KYC compliance and identity verification
  • Payment processing via aMember
  • Trader payouts via RISE WORKS
  • Platform improvement and analytics
  • Communication (notifications, support, marketing with consent)

4. Data Sharing

We share data only with:

  • Volumetrica: Trading platform — trade execution data
  • aMember: Payment processing — subscription and billing data
  • RISE WORKS: Trader payouts — payment and tax information
  • KYC provider: Identity verification documents

We never sell personal data to third parties.

5. Data Retention

  • Account data: Duration of the account + 5 years after deletion
  • Trading data: Archived indefinitely for scoring integrity
  • KYC documents: Duration required by applicable regulation
  • Payment records: 10 years (legal obligation)

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, rectify, delete, restrict processing, and port your personal data. You may also object to processing. To exercise these rights, contact contact@climbertrade.com.

7. Cookies

We use essential cookies for platform functionality and optional analytics cookies. You can manage your cookie preferences at any time through your browser settings.

8. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encryption, access controls, and regular security audits to protect your personal data.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated periodically. We will notify users of material changes via email or platform notification. For any questions, contact contact@climbertrade.com.
