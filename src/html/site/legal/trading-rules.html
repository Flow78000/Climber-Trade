1. Scope & Application

These Trading Regulations apply to all users of the Climber Trade platform, both during the Evaluation phase and the Live Trading phase. By subscribing to any Climber Trade service, the User acknowledges having read and accepted these regulations in full.

The regulations are complementary to the Terms & Conditions and the Professional Trader Contract. In case of conflict, the more restrictive rule applies.

2. Evaluation Phase — Capital & Account

  • Simulated capital: $1,000,000 USD (fictitious) for all evaluation accounts
  • Account currency: USD exclusively
  • Account limits: Maximum 1 account per natural person. An additional account is permitted only with proof of registered company ownership.
  • Risk: No real financial loss is possible during evaluation. Capital is 100% fictitious.

3. Authorized Instruments

3.1 Futures (CME / CBOT / NYMEX / COMEX / EUREX)

  • Indices: ES, NQ, YM, RTY, FDAX, FESX
  • Currencies: 6E, 6B, 6J, 6A, 6C
  • Commodities: CL, GC, SI, NG
  • Rates: ZN, ZB, ZF, ZT

3.2 Forex

28 major and minor currency pairs are authorized for trading.

3.3 Equities

  • US Markets: NYSE and NASDAQ listed stocks with market capitalization > $500M
  • ETFs: SPY, QQQ, TLT, GLD
  • Commissions: $0.005 per share (minimum $1 per order)

3.4 CFDs

  • Global indices (cash and futures-based)
  • Commodities (energy, metals, agriculture)
  • Cryptocurrency: BTC and ETH exclusively
Platform: All trading is executed through Volumetrica, the official trading platform of Climber Trade.

4. Authorized Trading Styles

The following trading styles and approaches are permitted on the platform:

  • Scalping
  • Day trading
  • Swing trading
  • Position trading
  • Algorithmic / HFT (High Frequency Trading)
  • Hedging
  • Arbitrage
  • News trading
  • AI-assisted trading
  • Multi-strategy approaches

5. Prohibited Practices

Violation of any prohibited practice results in immediate investigation and may lead to permanent exclusion.
  • Copy trading between accounts (own or third-party)
  • Insider trading — use of material non-public information
  • Coordinated group trading — organized trading rings or signal-sharing groups designed to manipulate scoring
  • Market manipulation — spoofing, layering, wash trading, or any form of artificial price influence
  • Latency arbitrage — exploiting infrastructure latency or platform pricing delays
  • Undeclared multiple accounts — operating more than the authorized number of accounts

6. Position Rules

6.1 Intraday Positions

No specific restrictions on intraday position sizing beyond the leverage scoring framework (optimal range: 2x–5x notional leverage).

6.2 Overnight Positions

  • Authorized if total portfolio exposure does not exceed 5:1 notional leverage

6.3 Weekend Positions

  • Authorized if total portfolio exposure does not exceed 1:1 notional leverage
  • Account balance must be ≥ $50,000 to hold weekend positions
Notional calculation: Notional = Contract value per point × Asset price. Example for NQ: $20 × 25,576 = $511,520 notional per contract.

7. Minimum Activity

Traders must maintain a minimum level of activity to keep their account in good standing:

  • Minimum 4 trading days per rolling 30-day period
  • A trading day is defined as any day with at least 1 trade executed
  • Failure to meet this requirement may result in a warning or account suspension

8. Live Phase — Risk Limits

The following automatic risk management triggers apply during the Live Trading phase:

ParameterThresholdAutomatic Action
Daily drawdown > 15% Alert Automatic prevention email sent to the Trader
Total drawdown > 20% VAR Alert Infraction notified; placed under surveillance
Intraday drawdown > 50% Stop Forced 24-hour trading suspension
Total drawdown > 80% Pause 5-day trading pause + return to scoring (30-day consolidation)
Score < 75 for 7 days Return Return to scoring; must consolidate ≥ 85 for 7 consecutive days
Score < 50 Freeze Live account frozen; mandatory return to evaluation

9. Live Phase — Exposure Limits

  • Overnight exposure: Maximum 5:1 notional leverage
  • Weekend exposure: Maximum 1:1 notional leverage + account balance ≥ $50,000

All other evaluation phase rules (instruments, styles, prohibited practices) apply equally during the Live phase.

10. Return to Evaluation

A Trader may be returned to the evaluation phase under the conditions specified in Section 8. To regain live access after a return:

  1. Continue trading in evaluation mode
  2. Restore CLIMB Score to ≥ 85/100
  3. Maintain the qualifying score for 7 consecutive trading days
  4. Complete any mandatory consolidation period (30 days if triggered by > 80% drawdown)

11. Sanctions Scale

Violations of these regulations are subject to a progressive sanctions framework:

LevelSanctionDetails
Level 1 Warning Written notification. The Trader has 48 hours to correct the violation.
Level 2 Suspension Temporary account suspension for 7 to 30 days depending on severity.
Level 3 Permanent Exclusion Definitive ban from the platform. All live capital is forfeited. No future access.
Severity determines entry point: Minor infractions begin at Level 1. Severe violations (fraud, market manipulation, insider trading) may result in immediate Level 3 action without prior warning.

12. Appeals Process

Any Trader subject to a sanction may submit an appeal under the following conditions:

  1. Submission deadline: 7 calendar days from the date of sanction notification
  2. Method: Written appeal sent to contact@climbertrade.com
  3. Review period: Climber Trade will review the appeal and issue a decision within 14 calendar days
  4. Final decision: The appeal decision is definitive and without further recourse

During the appeal process, the sanction remains in effect unless explicitly suspended by Climber Trade.
