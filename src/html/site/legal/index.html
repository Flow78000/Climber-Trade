Foundation

Key Principles

Non-negotiable commitments that govern every aspect of Climber Trade.

Pool Cannot Be Exhausted

The Cohort Factor (FC) auto-regulates allocation at any scale. The liquidity pool is mathematically protected against depletion regardless of trader count.

Real Capital Allocation

Live traders receive real capital from the liquidity pool based on their CLIMB Score. No simulated promises — genuine funding tied to verified performance.

Transparent Metrics

Every scoring metric is documented, explained, and visible in real time. The public pool dashboard shows total size, trader count, and anonymized distributions.

Pure Meritocracy

Only performance determines outcomes. Capital allocation, profit splits, and scaling are entirely driven by the CLIMB Score — no favoritism, no shortcuts.
