Contract Types

Standard vs Micro Contracts

Choose the contract size that matches your strategy and risk profile

Standard Contracts

Full-size futures contracts with higher margin requirements. The standard for professional and institutional traders operating with larger positions.

  • Full notional exposure per contract
  • Higher margin requirements
  • Maximum liquidity and tightest spreads
  • Ideal for professional-size positions

Micro Contracts

1/10th the size of standard contracts with proportionally lower margin. Micro contracts allow you to fine-tune your leverage and hit the 2x–5x sweet spot more precisely.

  • 1/10th the notional value of standard
  • Lower margin requirements
  • Precise position sizing and risk management
  • Granular leverage control for optimal CLIMB Score
Why Micros Matter for Your Score

The CLIMB Score rewards leverage between 2x and 5x with a perfect 100 pts. Micro contracts let you dial in exact exposure — instead of jumping from 2 ES contracts ($1,060,000) to 3 ($1,590,000), you can use MES to adjust in $53,000 increments for precision control.

Practical Example

Trading 5 NQ contracts at ~$420,000 notional each = $2,100,000 total exposure on a $1,000,000 account = 2.1x leverage = 100 pts

Formula: Notional = Point Value × Asset Price. Leverage = Total Notional / Account Balance.
