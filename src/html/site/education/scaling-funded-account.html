Scaling Your Funded Account: From $1M to Unlimited Capital

How Climber Trade's scaling system rewards consistency with ever-growing capital allocation.

March 2026 · 10 min read

The Problem With Fixed Capital Accounts

Most prop firms hand you a fixed account — $100K, $200K, maybe $500K — and that is where the story ends. No matter how well you perform, your capital stays the same. You might be delivering a 15% annual return with pristine risk management, but you are still trading the same size account you started with six months ago.

This model fundamentally misaligns incentives. If a trader is consistently profitable with excellent risk metrics, both the firm and the trader benefit from increasing that trader's allocation. Climber Trade built its entire funding model around this principle: your capital grows with your performance.

How Score-Based Allocation Works

At Climber Trade, your funded capital is not a fixed number. It is a percentage of the total liquidity pool, determined by your CLIMB Score. The formula that governs your allocation has multiple components, each designed to reward consistency and excellence.

Base Allocation: %Score

Your base allocation is calculated directly from your CLIMB Score. At the minimum funded threshold of 85, you receive 1.00% of the pool. For every point above 85, your allocation increases linearly up to 2.00% at a perfect score of 100. This means a trader with a score of 90 receives 1.33% of the pool, while a trader at 95 receives 1.67%.

The elegance of this system is that it responds in real time to your performance. Improve your score by five points and your capital allocation increases proportionally. Let your score slip and the allocation adjusts downward. The market is always telling you exactly where you stand.

The Cohort Factor: Built-In Protection

One concern traders raise is what happens when more people join the platform. Does everyone's allocation shrink to nothing? The Cohort Factor (FC) handles this elegantly. As the number of qualified traders (those scoring 50 or above) increases, the FC adjusts to prevent the pool from being overexposed. However, the pool itself also grows with more subscribers, creating a natural balance.

In practice, this means early traders with high scores benefit from larger relative allocations, while the system remains sustainable as the platform scales. The pool cannot be exhausted — it is mathematically self-regulating.

The Elite Pool: Double Allocation at 95+

Reaching a CLIMB Score of 95 or above unlocks access to the Elite Pool, which represents 10% of total pool capital. This is one of the most powerful scaling mechanisms available to funded traders.

Elite traders receive a dual allocation. They draw from both the Standard Pool (90% of capital, shared with all funded traders scoring 85+) and the Elite Pool (10% of capital, exclusive to traders scoring 95+). Since the Elite Pool is shared among fewer traders, the per-trader allocation from this pool tends to be significantly larger.

Consider a scenario: the total pool is $5 million. The Standard Pool is $4.5 million, the Elite Pool is $500K. If there are 50 funded traders but only 3 score above 95, each Elite trader draws from a $500K pool among just three participants — a substantial addition to their Standard allocation.

Maintaining Elite Status

Reaching 95 requires excellence across all four CLIMB Score pillars. Your risk management must be pristine, your performance metrics strong, your consistency impeccable, and your execution quality top-tier. The multiplicative nature of the scoring formula means weakness in any single area makes 95 very difficult to reach. This is by design — Elite status represents truly professional-grade trading.

The Evolution Prime: Score-Based Bonus

Beyond the base allocation, Climber Trade applies an evolution prime that adds a percentage bonus to your allocation based on how far above 85 your score sits. At 85, the bonus is 0%. It increases by 1% for each point above 85, capping at 10% when you reach 95 or higher.

This prime stacks with your base allocation. A trader at score 92 receives their base %Score allocation multiplied by (1 + 7%), giving them a 7% bump on top of their standard allocation. At 95+, the full 10% bonus applies, further amplifying the Elite advantage.

Seniority Bonus: Time Rewards Loyalty

Climber Trade recognizes that consistently funded traders represent the most valuable participants in the ecosystem. The Seniority Bonus adds an incremental monthly allocation increase, capped at 3% after six months in live trading.

This means a trader who has maintained funded status for six months receives 3% more capital on top of their score-based allocation and prime. The bonus grows each month, creating a compounding incentive to stay consistent over time rather than chasing short-term peaks.

The Complete Scaling Formula

When you combine all the components, the final allocation formula is:

Final Allocation = Pool Total x %Score x Cohort Factor x (1 + Prime + Seniority Bonus)

This formula creates a powerful growth engine. Every component rewards different aspects of trading excellence — raw score, performance above the minimum, and sustained consistency over time. There is no ceiling on total capital because the pool itself grows continuously.

Monthly Scaling in Practice

Scaling reviews happen monthly. At each review, the system recalculates your allocation based on your current CLIMB Score, the pool size, the cohort factor, and your accumulated seniority. If your score has improved, your next month's capital is larger. If the pool has grown from new subscribers and trading profits, your percentage translates to more dollars.

Compound Growth Math

Consider a trader who enters Live with a score of 87 and systematically improves by 1-2 points per month while the pool grows at 5% monthly. After six months:

  • Score progression: 87 → 89 → 91 → 92 → 93 → 94
  • Base allocation increase: From 1.13% to 1.60% — a 41% increase in allocation percentage
  • Prime increase: From 2% to 9% — a significant multiplier boost
  • Seniority: 3% bonus accumulated over six months
  • Pool growth: If the pool grew 30% over six months, the dollar value of each percentage point is also 30% higher

The combined effect is that this trader's actual capital allocation may have grown by 80% or more over six months, even though each individual component changed modestly. This is the power of compounding across multiple growth vectors.

Profit Splits Scale Too

Capital allocation is only half the story. Your profit split also improves with your score. At 85-89, the split is 50/50 between trader and Climber. At 90-94, it shifts to 55/45 in the trader's favor. At 95-100 (Elite), the split becomes 60/40.

This means a higher score gives you both more capital AND a larger share of the profits generated from that capital. The dollar impact of moving from 87 to 95 is far greater than the eight points might suggest.

What Stops the Scaling

The system also has built-in brakes. If your CLIMB Score drops below 75 for seven consecutive days, you return to the evaluation scoring phase. Below 50, your live account is frozen entirely. These thresholds ensure that only traders maintaining professional standards continue to receive capital.

Activity requirements also apply. You must trade a minimum of 4 days out of every 30 to maintain your funded status. This prevents traders from parking capital without generating returns for the pool.

Strategies for Maximizing Your Scale

  1. Focus on consistency over home runs. The CLIMB Score rewards steady daily returns between 0% and 1% of capital. Occasional large wins actually hurt your ABS_NPL score due to the diminishing returns above 1%.
  2. Keep leverage in the optimal zone. The Average Lot Number metric scores highest at 2x-5x notional. Staying in this range keeps one of your four pillar scores high without taking excessive risk.
  3. Maintain stable position sizing. The Lot Stability Ratio penalizes erratic sizing. Use consistent lot sizes across trades to keep this metric strong.
  4. Recover from drawdowns quickly. The R_T_AFD metric rewards fast recovery. If you take a loss, do not go on tilt — methodically work back to your equity high.
  5. Think in months, not days. Seniority bonuses and sustained high scores compound over time. A trader who maintains 90 for six months will outperform a trader who spikes to 95 and falls back to 82.
