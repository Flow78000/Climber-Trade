Why Climber Trade Works Differently

Traditional prop firms — including Purdia Capital — operate on a pass/fail model: you pay a fee, attempt to hit a profit target within drawdown limits, and either pass or lose your fee. Climber Trade replaces this with a continuous algorithmic evaluation called the CLIMB Score.

The CLIMB Score measures 4 pillars of trading quality: Risk Management, Performance, Consistency, and Quality Execution. You are scored in real time across 13 metrics. Reach 85/100 and maintain it for 7 consecutive trading days — you get funded. No profit target. No daily stop-out rule. No arbitrary pass/fail.

The funded account is $1,000,000 for every trader — regardless of plan. Unlike Purdia Capital's tiered accounts ($25K, $50K, $100K, $150K), Climber gives the same capital to all traders. Your profit split then scales from 50% to 60% based on your ongoing CLIMB Score, meaning the better you trade, the more you earn.
