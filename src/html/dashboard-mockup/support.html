FAQ
How does the CLIMB Score work? ⌄
The CLIMB Score is a composite metric calculated from 4 pillars: Risk Management (×4), Performance (×4), Consistency & Style (×3), and Quality Execution (multiplicateur). Each pillar contains multiple sub-metrics. The score ranges from 0–100. A score ≥85 over 7 consecutive trading days after 30 days of evaluation makes you eligible for live funding.
When can I access live funding? ⌄
You must meet all four criteria simultaneously: CLIMB Score ≥85/100, maintained for 7 consecutive trading days (with at least 1 trade per day), at least 30 calendar days since account creation, and completed KYC verification. Once all criteria are met, activation occurs within 24–48 hours on weekdays.
How do payouts work? ⌄
Payouts are processed on the 5th and 20th of each month automatically, or on demand with 24h notice. Minimum withdrawal is $500. Payments are made via Rise Works wallet within 3–5 business days. Only closed, consolidated positions are eligible for withdrawal.
